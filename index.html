Architecture & Delivery Scorecard

A free, browser-based health check for your system and the way you ship it. Answer 20 questions about deployments, monitoring, backups and access to production, and get a score per area, your three biggest risks and what to do about them.

  • About 5 minutes · 20 questions in 5 areas
  • Private · nothing is sent anywhere, it all runs in your browser
  • Rule-based · no AI, so you can see exactly how the score is built

How it works

No AI, just rules
Every answer maps to a fixed score from 0 to 3. Each question has a weight, and your score is the weighted share of the points available, per area and overall. The same answers always give the same result. Your biggest risks are the questions with the largest weighted gap to the best answer.
Private by design
There are no accounts, cookies, analytics or third-party requests. Your answers stay in this browser (local storage, so you can resume) and are never uploaded. A result link contains your answers in the part of the URL after the #, which browsers do not send to servers.
What it is, and is not
A quick, honest self-assessment that surfaces common gaps. It is not an audit: it cannot see your real system, so treat the output as a starting point for a conversation, not a verdict.
